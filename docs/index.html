WC2026 Predictions Dashboard

Every probability shown here is generated by a custom simulation model - running thousands of tournament scenarios to estimate how far each team is likely to go.

Full methodology, explanations, and project details are available in arbelna/World-Cup-2026-Bracket.

Sections

Probability Table

Shows every team’s model-estimated probability of reaching each knockout stage - from Round of 32 through the Final and Winner. Sort by any stage column, filter by confederation or group, and search by team name to compare teams side by side.

Basket Analysis

Pick up to 8 teams and see how many are expected to advance to a chosen stage. Shows the full probability distribution - both exact and cumulative - so you can answer questions like "what are the odds at least 3 of these 5 teams reach the QF?"

Top Configurations

Lists the most likely exact combinations of teams to reach a given stage. Useful for seeing which clusters of teams dominate the model’s simulations and how much of the total probability is concentrated in the top-ranked outcomes.
